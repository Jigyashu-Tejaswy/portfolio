Research Experience

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Option Pricing under Jump-Diffusion

Independent Research Project, IIIT Gwalior
PythonPyTorch NumPySciPy Matplotlib
Jun 2026 – Present

Built a mesh-free physics-informed neural network (PINN) in PyTorch to solve the partial integro-differential equation governing European option pricing under the Merton and Kou jump-diffusion models, covering 5 benchmark experiments.

Resolved the non-local jump integral by discretising with composite-trapezoidal quadrature (200 nodes), reducing the PIDE to a PDE residual trainable end-to-end with full-batch L-BFGS.

Achieved 1.6e-08 MSE on smooth benchmarks — 6.4× lower error than a baseline 256×256 finite-difference solver — over a 128×128 grid of 16,384 test points.

Extracted risk sensitivities (Delta, Gamma, Theta) via automatic differentiation with zero extra training; shipped 14 modular components (~1,000 LOC) with reproducible experiment runners.
